Questions

How does booking work?

Choose your dates, items and delivery option above, then send a request. We'll check everything and message you to confirm, with the final total and how to pay.

How much is delivery?

It's based on how far you are from us and covers both drop-off and collection. Enter your postcode in the booking form for an instant estimate. We usually deliver within about 30 miles. If you're further away, send a request anyway and we'll let you know. Or collect from us for free.

Is there a deposit?

Yes, a refundable deposit of 10% of the hire cost. You get it back in full once everything's collected in good condition. We may keep some or all of it to cover damage or loss.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery or collection, or bank transfer. We send our bank details when we confirm your booking. Nothing is charged when you send a request.

How long can I hire for?

From a single day up to 14 days. Prices are per item, per day, so a two-day hire costs twice a one-day hire.

Can I check on my booking?

Yes. After sending a request you'll get a reference and a link to a page showing your booking's status.
